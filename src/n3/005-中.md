# ～中

**Meaning** · a suffix meaning "in the middle of" (read <b>ちゅう</b>) or "all through, all over" (read <b>じゅう</b>)

This is not the noun {f|中|なか}. It is a suffix that glues straight onto the back of
another noun with no `の`, and it comes in two flavours that share one kanji:

- <b>ちゅう</b> - something is **in progress**, or a period is a **deadline** you must
  act inside. {f|会議|かいぎ}{f|中|ちゅう}, {f|午前|ごぜん}{f|中|ちゅう}に.
- <b>じゅう</b> - a span or a space is covered **from end to end**.
  {f|一日|いちにち}{f|中|じゅう}, {f|世界|せかい}{f|中|じゅう}.

## Formation

Nothing conjugates; you are building a compound noun. What varies is the reading and
whether `に` follows.

| Attaches to | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Activity noun → in progress | Noun + {f|中|ちゅう} | {f|工事|こうじ} → {f|工事|こうじ}{f|中|ちゅう} |
| Period → deadline | Noun + {f|中|ちゅう}に / {f|中|じゅう}に | {f|午前|ごぜん} → {f|午前|ごぜん}{f|中|ちゅう}に |
| Period → whole extent | Noun + {f|中|じゅう} | {f|一年|いちねん} → {f|一年|いちねん}{f|中|じゅう} |
| Place or body → whole extent | Noun + {f|中|じゅう} | {f|世界|せかい} → {f|世界|せかい}{f|中|じゅう} |
| As a predicate | Noun + {f|中|ちゅう}だ／です | {f|使用|しよう} → {f|使用|しよう}{f|中|ちゅう}です |

<div class="note">

**Note** - the reading is a property of the word, not something you derive. Learn these
in two lists.

<b>ちゅう</b>: {f|会議|かいぎ}{f|中|ちゅう}, {f|食事|しょくじ}{f|中|ちゅう},
{f|授業|じゅぎょう}{f|中|ちゅう}, {f|電話|でんわ}{f|中|ちゅう},
{f|営業|えいぎょう}{f|中|ちゅう}, {f|使用|しよう}{f|中|ちゅう},
{f|工事|こうじ}{f|中|ちゅう}, {f|午前|ごぜん}{f|中|ちゅう},
{f|今月|こんげつ}{f|中|ちゅう}に

<b>じゅう</b>: {f|一日|いちにち}{f|中|じゅう}, {f|一年|いちねん}{f|中|じゅう},
{f|今日|きょう}{f|中|じゅう}に, {f|世界|せかい}{f|中|じゅう},
{f|町|まち}{f|中|じゅう}, {f|家|いえ}{f|中|じゅう}, {f|体|からだ}{f|中|じゅう}

</div>

## Examples

<pre>
{f|父|ちち}は{f|今|いま}{f|電話|でんわ}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>です。
My father is on the phone at the moment.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|昨日|きのう}は{f|一日|いちにち}<b>{f|中|じゅう}</b>{f|雨|あめ}だった。
It rained all day yesterday.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|授業|じゅぎょう}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>はスマホをかばんにしまってください。
Please keep your phone in your bag during class.
</pre>

<pre>
この{f|温泉|おんせん}は{f|一年|いちねん}<b>{f|中|じゅう}</b>{f|営業|えいぎょう}しています。
This hot spring is open for business all year round.
</pre>

<pre>
そのニュースはあっという{f|間|ま}に{f|世界|せかい}<b>{f|中|じゅう}</b>に{f|広|ひろ}がった。
The news spread around the world in no time.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|昨日|きのう}は{f|体|からだ}<b>{f|中|じゅう}</b>が{f|痛|いた}くて、{f|何|なに}もする{f|気|き}が{f|起|お}きなかった。
I ached all over yesterday and couldn't summon the will to do anything.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|工事|こうじ}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>のため、こちらの{f|入|い}り{f|口|ぐち}はお{f|使|つか}いいただけません。
This entrance is out of use owing to construction work.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|午前|ごぜん}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>は{f|会議|かいぎ}が{f|入|はい}っておりますので、{f|午後|ごご}にもう{f|一度|いちど}お{f|電話|でんわ}いただけますか。
I'm in meetings all morning, so could I ask you to call again in the afternoon?
</pre>

## Nuance & register

The suffix itself is neutral, but the words it builds cluster in particular places.
<code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code> compounds are the language of **signs and status messages**:
{f|営業|えいぎょう}{f|中|ちゅう} on a shop door, {f|使用|しよう}{f|中|ちゅう} on a toilet
lock, {f|準備|じゅんび}{f|中|ちゅう} in a restaurant window,
{f|会議|かいぎ}{f|中|ちゅう} on a colleague's chat status. Learning to read them is worth
more than learning to produce them.

<code>{f|中|じゅう}</code> compounds lean the other way: they are emphatic and slightly
emotional, and they turn up in complaints and enthusiasm alike - 
{f|部屋|へや}{f|中|じゅう}ほこりだらけだ, {f|町|まち}{f|中|じゅう}{f|探|さが}した.
The nuance is "and I mean *everywhere*", so a flat, factual sentence often reads better
with {f|全部|ぜんぶ} or ずっと instead.

Two things about the particle. When <code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code>/<code>{f|中|じゅう}</code>
names the window an action must land in, `に` is required:
{f|今日|きょう}{f|中|じゅう}に{f|送|おく}ります. When it merely describes the background,
`に` is absent, and `は` often appears instead:
{f|授業|じゅぎょう}{f|中|ちゅう}は… - this is the same window-filling logic as
<code>{f|間|あいだ}</code> without `に`.

One reading to flag honestly: for {f|今週|こんしゅう} and {f|今月|こんげつ} both
readings circulate, and dictionaries generally give ちゅう - 
{f|今月|こんげつ}{f|中|ちゅう}に. For {f|今日|きょう} the reading is solidly じゅう. If
you hear a native speaker say it the other way round, they are not wrong; this is a
genuinely unsettled corner.

## Contrast

### {f|中|ちゅう} vs {f|中|じゅう}

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - <code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code> looks at a **point inside** a period:
something is going on, or must be finished, within it. <code>{f|中|じゅう}</code> sweeps
the **whole extent**: nothing is left out.

</div>

<pre>
{f|今日|きょう}<b>{f|中|じゅう}に</b>{f|見積|みつ}もりをお{f|送|おく}りします。
I'll send the quote before the day is out.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|日曜日|にちようび}は{f|一日|いちにち}<b>{f|中|じゅう}</b>{f|寝|ね}ていた。
On Sunday I was asleep the whole day.
</pre>

Note that {f|今日|きょう}{f|中|じゅう} takes じゅう even though it is a deadline - the
reading follows the *word*, not the meaning. The pattern is only a tendency.

Where a noun genuinely takes both, the meanings separate cleanly:
{f|試験|しけん}{f|中|ちゅう} is "the exam is under way", while
{f|夏|なつ}{f|中|じゅう} is "all summer long". You will not find a word that means both
at once.

### ～{f|中|ちゅう}に vs ～のうちに

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - <code>{f|中|ちゅう}に</code> is a **limit**, usually one somebody
else set. <code>のうちに</code> is an **opportunity** you are choosing to use.

</div>

<pre>
レポートは{f|今月|こんげつ}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}に</b>{f|提出|ていしゅつ}してください。
Please hand the report in by the end of this month.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|来月|らいげつ}は{f|出張|しゅっちょう}が{f|多|おお}いから、{f|今月|こんげつ}<b>のうちに</b>{f|書|か}いてしまおう。
I've a lot of business trips next month, so let's get it written while we still have this month.
</pre>

The first is an instruction; the second is a plan with a reason attached. There is also
a hard structural difference: <code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code> takes only nouns naming a
measured span, while <code>うちに</code> takes clauses, adjectives and states - 
{f|元気|げんき}なうちに has no <code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code> equivalent at all.

### ～{f|中|ちゅう} vs ～の{f|最中|さいちゅう}に

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - <code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code> just says the activity is on.
<code>{f|最中|さいちゅう}に</code> says you are in the **thick** of it, and something has
cut across it.

</div>

<pre>
{f|会議|かいぎ}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>ですので、{f|後|あと}でかけ{f|直|なお}します。
I'm in a meeting, so I'll call back later.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|会議|かいぎ}の<b>{f|最中|さいちゅう}に</b>{f|停電|ていでん}して、{f|全部|ぜんぶ}やり{f|直|なお}しになった。
The power went out in the middle of the meeting and we had to start over.
</pre>

<code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code> is a neutral status label and works fine as a whole predicate.
<code>{f|最中|さいちゅう}に</code> is dramatic and expects an interruption to follow.
Note the different attachment too: <code>{f|会議|かいぎ}{f|中|ちゅう}</code> with no `の`,
but <code>{f|会議|かいぎ}の{f|最中|さいちゅう}</code> with one.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ {f|会議|かいぎ}<b>の{f|中|ちゅう}</b>に{f|電話|でんわ}が{f|来|き}ました。 → ✅ {f|会議|かいぎ}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>に{f|電話|でんわ}が{f|来|き}ました。
  This <code>中</code> is a suffix and takes no `の`. Writing
  {f|会議|かいぎ}の{f|中|なか}で changes the word to なか and means "within the content
  of the meeting".

- ❌ {f|一日|いちにち}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>{f|歩|ある}きました。 → ✅ {f|一日|いちにち}<b>{f|中|じゅう}</b>{f|歩|ある}きました。
  "All day" is the whole-extent sense, which is じゅう. The kanji gives you no clue, so
  the reading has to be learned with the word.

- ❌ {f|今日|きょう}{f|中|じゅう}<b>で</b>{f|終|お}わらせます。 → ✅ {f|今日|きょう}{f|中|じゅう}<b>に</b>{f|終|お}わらせます。
  A window that an action must land inside takes `に`, exactly as with
  <code>{f|間|あいだ}に</code>. `で` would be marking a place or a means.

- ❌ {f|弟|おとうと}は{f|勉強|べんきょう}{f|中|ちゅう}<b>しています</b>。 → ✅ {f|弟|おとうと}は{f|勉強|べんきょう}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}です</b>。
  <code>{f|中|ちゅう}</code> makes a **noun**, so it ends in だ／です, not in a verb. Say
  either {f|勉強|べんきょう}{f|中|ちゅう}です or {f|勉強|べんきょう}しています, never both.

## Practice

**1.** すみません、{f|山田|やまだ}は{f|今|いま}{f|外出|がいしゅつ}____です。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|山田|やまだ}は{f|今|いま}{f|外出|がいしゅつ}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>です。

{f|外出|がいしゅつ} is an activity currently under way, which is the ちゅう sense, and
the suffix attaches with no `の`. This is the standard way of telling a caller that
somebody is out of the office.

</details>

**2.** Choose: {f|夏|なつ}になると、この{f|川|かわ}には（{f|一日|いちにち}{f|中|ちゅう}・{f|一日|いちにち}{f|中|じゅう}）{f|子|こ}どもが{f|集|あつ}まる。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|一日|いちにち}<b>{f|中|じゅう}</b>{f|子|こ}どもが{f|集|あつ}まる。

The sentence covers the whole of the day from end to end, which is the じゅう sense, and
{f|一日|いちにち}{f|中|じゅう} is fixed in that reading. ❌{f|一日|いちにち}{f|中|ちゅう}
is not an alternative pronunciation of the same phrase - it is simply wrong.

</details>

**3.** Translate into Japanese: "Could you reply by the end of today? I'm out all morning tomorrow."

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|今日|きょう}<b>{f|中|じゅう}に</b>お{f|返事|へんじ}をいただけますか。{f|明日|あした}の{f|午前|ごぜん}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>は{f|外|そと}に{f|出|で}ておりますので。

Both halves use the suffix, with different readings and different jobs.
{f|今日|きょう}{f|中|じゅう}に is a deadline, so it takes `に`;
{f|午前|ごぜん}{f|中|ちゅう}は describes a window that the absence fills, so it takes `は`
and no `に`.

</details>

**4.** Find and fix the error: この{f|店|みせ}は{f|準備|じゅんび}の{f|中|ちゅう}なので、まだ{f|入|はい}れません。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

✅ この{f|店|みせ}は<b>{f|準備|じゅんび}{f|中|ちゅう}</b>なので、まだ{f|入|はい}れません。

The `の` has to go: this <code>中</code> welds directly onto the noun in front of it. With `の` the
kanji would be read なか and the phrase would mean "inside the preparations", which is
not a thing.

</details>

**5.** Explain the difference in nuance:
(a) {f|旅行|りょこう}<b>{f|中|ちゅう}</b>に{f|財布|さいふ}をなくした。
(b) {f|旅行|りょこう}の<b>{f|間|あいだ}</b>ずっと{f|天気|てんき}がよかった。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) uses the suffix to name a period the trip occupied and drops a single event into it,
so it behaves exactly like <code>{f|間|あいだ}に</code>: the wallet was lost at one moment
somewhere inside the trip. The `に` is what places the event.

(b) has no `に` because the good weather lasted the whole trip. You could equally write
{f|旅行|りょこう}{f|中|ちゅう}ずっと{f|天気|てんき}がよかった - the suffix works there too,
and the absence of `に` is doing the same job in both patterns.

</details>
