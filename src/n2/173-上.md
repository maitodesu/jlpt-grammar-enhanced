# ～上 - in terms of

**Meaning** · in terms of ~; as far as ~ is concerned - read じょう, welded onto the end of a noun

This character is read <b>じょう</b> here, not うえ. Stuck onto a noun of Chinese origin it turns
it into a dimension along which something holds:
{f|法律|ほうりつ}{f|上|じょう} "legally", {f|安全|あんぜん}{f|上|じょう} "as regards
safety", {f|立場|たちば}{f|上|じょう} "given the position I'm in". It is the most
compressed way Japanese has of saying "on the ~ front", and it belongs to formal
prose and workplace speech.

## Formation

There is no gap and no particle between the noun and the suffix.

| Attaches to | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Sino-Japanese noun | Noun + {f|上|じょう} | {f|法律|ほうりつ} → {f|法律|ほうりつ}{f|上|じょう} |
| With topic / contrast | Noun + {f|上|じょう}は | {f|計算|けいさん} → {f|計算|けいさん}{f|上|じょう}は |
| Modifying a following noun | Noun + {f|上|じょう}の | {f|健康|けんこう} → {f|健康|けんこう}{f|上|じょう}の{f|理由|りゆう} |
| Adverbially, before a predicate | Noun + {f|上|じょう} + predicate | {f|安全|あんぜん} → {f|安全|あんぜん}{f|上|じょう}{f|問題|もんだい}がある |
| As a set adverb | {f|経験|けいけん} / {f|立場|たちば} + {f|上|じょう}、… | {f|経験|けいけん} → {f|経験|けいけん}{f|上|じょう}、… |

<div class="note">

**Note** - this is not a productive suffix. It lives in a fixed set of compounds:
{f|法律|ほうりつ}{f|上|じょう}, {f|教育|きょういく}{f|上|じょう},
{f|健康|けんこう}{f|上|じょう}, {f|安全|あんぜん}{f|上|じょう},
{f|仕事|しごと}{f|上|じょう}, {f|立場|たちば}{f|上|じょう},
{f|経験|けいけん}{f|上|じょう}, {f|計算|けいさん}{f|上|じょう},
{f|規則|きそく}{f|上|じょう}, {f|表面|ひょうめん}{f|上|じょう},
{f|事実|じじつ}{f|上|じょう}, {f|歴史|れきし}{f|上|じょう},
{f|都合|つごう}{f|上|じょう}. Learn these; do not coin new ones.

</div>

## Nuance & register

Formal and compact. You will meet it constantly in notices, contracts, manuals,
news writing and the more careful register of office speech, and almost never in
relaxed conversation, where {f|健康|けんこう}のために or
{f|安全|あんぜん}のことを{f|考|かんが}えると do the same work at greater length.

Two of the compounds are worth singling out. {f|立場|たちば}{f|上|じょう} is the
standard polite way to decline to say or do something because of your role - it
appeals to a constraint rather than to a preference, and so avoids blame.
{f|表面|ひょうめん}{f|上|じょう}は, by contrast, is almost always the setup for a
"but": it flags the surface appearance precisely in order to contradict it.

The reading trap is worth stating plainly. The same character is うえ in
{f|机|つくえ}の{f|上|うえ}, あ in {f|上|あ}がる, のぼ in {f|上|のぼ}る, and じょう
here. Reading {f|仕事|しごと}{f|上|じょう} as しごとうえ is a very common learner
error and marks the sentence immediately.

## Contrast

### {f|上|じょう} vs {f|上|うえ}で

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - じょう means "in the ~ dimension"; うえで means "after doing"
or, literally, "on top of".

</div>

<pre>
{f|規則|きそく}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>、その{f|申|もう}し{f|込|こ}みは{f|受|う}け{f|付|つ}けられません。
Under the rules, we cannot accept that application.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|規則|きそく}を{f|確|たし}かめた<b>{f|上|うえ}で</b>、お{f|返事|へんじ}します。
I'll reply once I've checked the rules.
</pre>

Same character, unrelated grammar. じょう attaches to a bare noun with nothing
between; うえで follows a た-form verb or a noun + の, and always concerns
sequence. If there is a verb in front, the reading is うえ.

### {f|上|じょう} vs から{f|言|い}うと

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - both name a criterion. じょう is a **fixed compound** and
terse; <code>から{f|言|い}うと</code> is **free** and can take any noun.

</div>

<pre>
{f|安全|あんぜん}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>、ヘルメットの{f|着用|ちゃくよう}が{f|必要|ひつよう}です。
For safety, helmets must be worn.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|作業|さぎょう}の{f|効率|こうりつ}<b>から{f|言|い}うと</b>、この{f|方法|ほうほう}は{f|悪|わる}くない。
In terms of working efficiency, this method isn't bad.
</pre>

You cannot say ✗{f|効率|こうりつ}{f|上|じょう} freely just because
{f|安全|あんぜん}{f|上|じょう} exists - though a handful, like
{f|計算|けいさん}{f|上|じょう}, are established. When in doubt about whether a
compound exists, use <code>から{f|言|い}うと</code>, which never fails.

### {f|上|じょう} vs {f|的|てき}に

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - <code>{f|上|じょう}</code> names the **domain a fact belongs
to**; <code>{f|的|てき}に</code> names the **manner or angle** of a description.

</div>

<pre>
{f|健康|けんこう}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>の{f|問題|もんだい}で{f|欠席|けっせき}します。
I'll be absent because of a health problem.
</pre>

<pre>
この{f|食事|しょくじ}は{f|健康|けんこう}{f|的|てき}だ。
This meal is healthy.
</pre>

{f|健康|けんこう}{f|上|じょう}の{f|問題|もんだい} is a problem located in the health
department; {f|健康|けんこう}{f|的|てき} describes something as being of a healthy
character. They are not substitutes: ✗{f|健康|けんこう}{f|的|てき}な
{f|理由|りゆう}で{f|辞|や}めた does not mean what the learner intends.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ {f|気持|きも}ち<b>{f|上|じょう}</b> → ✅ {f|気持|きも}ちの<b>{f|上|うえ}</b>では
  This suffix attaches to Sino-Japanese nouns, not to native words like
  {f|気持|きも}ち.

- ❌ {f|健康|けんこう}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>な{f|理由|りゆう} → ✅ {f|健康|けんこう}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>の{f|理由|りゆう}
  <code>{f|上|じょう}</code> forms a noun, so it links to a following noun with の.

- ❌ {f|仕事|しごと}の<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>の{f|都合|つごう} → ✅ {f|仕事|しごと}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>の{f|都合|つごう}
  Nothing comes between the noun and the suffix - no の, no で, no を.

- ❌ reading {f|仕事|しごと}{f|上|うえ} → ✅ {f|仕事|しごと}{f|上|じょう}
  In this pattern the character is always じょう. うえ belongs to
  {f|机|つくえ}の{f|上|うえ} and to <code>～た{f|上|うえ}で</code>.

## Practice

**1.** {f|教育|きょういく}____、この{f|番組|ばんぐみ}は{f|子|こ}どもに{f|見|み}せたくない。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|教育|きょういく}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>、この{f|番組|ばんぐみ}は{f|子|こ}どもに{f|見|み}せたくない。

{f|教育|きょういく}{f|上|じょう} is one of the established compounds, and it names
the dimension - the educational one - along which the objection is being raised.

</details>

**2.** Choose: {f|書類|しょるい}を{f|確認|かくにん}した（{f|上|じょう}・{f|上|うえ}）で、{f|正式|せいしき}に{f|返事|へんじ}します。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|書類|しょるい}を{f|確認|かくにん}した<b>{f|上|うえ}</b>で、{f|正式|せいしき}に{f|返事|へんじ}します。

A た-form verb precedes it, so this is the "after doing" one, read うえ. The じょう
reading only follows a bare noun.

</details>

**3.** Translate into Japanese: "For reasons connected with my job, I can't say who the client is."

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|仕事|しごと}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>の{f|理由|りゆう}で、お{f|客様|きゃくさま}のお{f|名前|なまえ}は{f|申|もう}し{f|上|あ}げられません。

{f|仕事|しごと}{f|上|じょう}の{f|理由|りゆう} is the natural compression of "reasons
connected with my job", and the の is required before {f|理由|りゆう}.

</details>

**4.** Find and fix the error: {f|安全|あんぜん}の{f|上|じょう}で、ここでは{f|走|はし}らないでください。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

✅ {f|安全|あんぜん}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>、ここでは{f|走|はし}らないでください。

Both the の and the で are intrusions. The suffix attaches straight to
{f|安全|あんぜん}, and the resulting phrase can stand adverbially in front of the
main clause with only a comma.

</details>

**5.** Explain the difference in nuance:
(a) {f|表面|ひょうめん}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>は{f|問題|もんだい}がない。
(b) {f|事実|じじつ}<b>{f|上|じょう}</b>{f|問題|もんだい}がない。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) restricts the clean bill of health to appearances and all but announces a
"but" - {f|表面|ひょうめん}{f|上|じょう}は is used almost exclusively to set up a
contradiction underneath.

(b) says the opposite kind of thing: whatever the formal or nominal situation may
be, in practical fact there is no problem. {f|事実|じじつ}{f|上|じょう} means "to
all intents and purposes", and it strengthens the claim rather than hedging it.

</details>
