# ～て以来

**Meaning** · ever since - from one definite past event, right through to now

<code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> plants a flag at a single moment in the past and then
reports what has been true of the whole stretch between that moment and the
present. It is not a way of saying "and then"; it is a way of saying "and it has
been like that ever since".

## Formation

<code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> is a noun. It takes either a て-form clause or a bare
event noun in front of it - never a dictionary form, and never `の`.

| Attaches to | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Verb | て-form + {f|以来|いらい} | {f|入社|にゅうしゃ}する → {f|入社|にゅうしゃ}して{f|以来|いらい} |
| Verb ({f|来|く}る) | {f|来|き}て + {f|以来|いらい} | {f|日本|にほん}に{f|来|く}る → {f|日本|にほん}に{f|来|き}て{f|以来|いらい} |
| Noun (an event) | Noun + {f|以来|いらい} | {f|卒業|そつぎょう} → {f|卒業|そつぎょう}{f|以来|いらい} |
| Noun (a time point) | Noun + {f|以来|いらい} | {f|去年|きょねん}の{f|春|はる} → {f|去年|きょねん}の{f|春|はる}{f|以来|いらい} |
| Demonstrative | それ + {f|以来|いらい} | それ{f|以来|いらい}、{f|連絡|れんらく}がない |
| ❌ Dictionary form | ❌{f|卒業|そつぎょう}する{f|以来|いらい} | ✅{f|卒業|そつぎょう}して{f|以来|いらい} |
| ❌ Noun + の | ❌{f|卒業|そつぎょう}の{f|以来|いらい} | ✅{f|卒業|そつぎょう}{f|以来|いらい} |
| ❌ Future starting point | ❌{f|来月|らいげつ}{f|入社|にゅうしゃ}して{f|以来|いらい} | ✅{f|来月|らいげつ}{f|入社|にゅうしゃ}してから |

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - three conditions. The starting event happened **once**, in the
**past**, and the stretch it opens runs **up to now**. Fail any one and you want
`てから`.

</div>

<div class="note">

**Note** - the main clause normally says what has held true across that whole
stretch: a continuing state, an unbroken habit, or something that has *never*
happened ({f|一度|いちど}も…ていない). A single later incident is possible, but only
when it is measured against the span - {f|日本|にほん}に{f|来|き}て{f|以来|いらい}、{f|初|はじ}めて{f|雪|ゆき}を{f|見|み}た.

</div>

## Examples

<pre>
{f|入社|にゅうしゃ}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|一度|いちど}も{f|遅刻|ちこく}していない。
I haven't been late once since I joined the company.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|去年|きょねん}{f|引|ひ}っ{f|越|こ}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|彼|かれ}とは{f|会|あ}っていない。
I haven't seen him since I moved last year.
</pre>

<pre>
あの{f|映画|えいが}を{f|見|み}<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|飛行機|ひこうき}が{f|苦手|にがて}になった。
Ever since I saw that film, flying has made me uneasy.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|祖母|そぼ}は{f|退院|たいいん}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|毎朝|まいあさ}の{f|散歩|さんぽ}を{f|欠|か}かさない。
Since she came out of hospital, my grandmother hasn't missed a single morning walk.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|開店|かいてん}<b>{f|以来|いらい}</b>、この{f|店|みせ}の{f|味|あじ}は{f|変|か}わっていません。
The flavour at this shop hasn't changed since the day it opened.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|大学|だいがく}を{f|卒業|そつぎょう}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|同級生|どうきゅうせい}とのやり{f|取|と}りは{f|年賀状|ねんがじょう}だけになってしまった。
Since I graduated, my contact with my classmates has shrunk to New Year cards.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|震災|しんさい}<b>{f|以来|いらい}</b>、この{f|地区|ちく}では{f|防災|ぼうさい}{f|訓練|くんれん}が{f|年|ねん}に{f|二回|にかい}{f|行|おこな}われている。
Since the earthquake, disaster drills have been held twice a year in this district.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|一度|いちど}{f|痛|いた}い{f|目|め}に{f|遭|あ}っ<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|彼女|かのじょ}は{f|知|し}らない{f|人|ひと}の{f|話|はなし}をすぐには{f|信|しん}じなくなった。
Ever since she got burned once, she has stopped taking strangers at their word.
</pre>

## Nuance & register

<code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> leans written and formal. It is at home in a report, a
profile, a news paragraph or a careful spoken account; in relaxed chat most people
say `てから` instead. Nothing about it is stiff enough to sound wrong in speech,
but if you use it for the ten minutes since lunch it will sound comically grand.

The span matters. <code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> wants a *long* one - months and years,
not hours. This is why it so often pairs with {f|一度|いちど}も, ずっと, まだ and a
negative: the whole point is that a great deal of time has gone by and the
situation has not moved.

There is a strong pull toward outcomes the speaker did not choose. A change in
health, a change of feeling, a habit that stuck, contact that dried up - these are
the natural main clauses. Announcing your own deliberate plan after
<code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> is odd, because a plan belongs to a moment, not to a
stretch of years.

<code>それ{f|以来|いらい}</code> is worth learning as a unit. Dropped at the head of a
sentence it means "and it has been that way ever since", and it is one of the most
useful connectives in narration: 「{f|一度|いちど}{f|断|ことわ}られた。それ{f|以来|いらい}、{f|誘|さそ}っていない。」

## Contrast

### ～て{f|以来|いらい} vs ～てから

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - `てから` orders two events. <code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> opens a
span and describes the whole of it.

</div>

`てから` is the workhorse, and it is neutral about how much time passed, whether the
span reaches the present, and whether the starting event is unique. It also happily
takes a future starting point.

<pre>
{f|薬|くすり}を{f|飲|の}ん<b>でから</b>、30{f|分|ぷん}{f|横|よこ}になってください。
Lie down for thirty minutes after taking the medicine.
</pre>

Swap in <code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> there and the sentence collapses: you cannot issue
an instruction about a stretch of years that has already gone by.

<pre>
{f|手術|しゅじゅつ}を{f|受|う}け<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|体調|たいちょう}がずっといい。
I've been in good shape ever since I had the operation.
</pre>

Here `てから` would also be grammatical, and slightly flatter - it would sequence the
operation and the good health rather than presenting the good health as the
character of everything since.

### ～て{f|以来|いらい} vs {f|間|あいだ} / {f|間|あいだ}に

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - {f|間|あいだ} fences off an interval with **both** ends fixed.
<code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> fixes only the **start** and leaves the far end open at
the present moment.

</div>

This is the N3 pattern <code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> is closest to, and the divergence is
exactly that missing right-hand edge.

<pre>
{f|留学|りゅうがく}している<b>{f|間|あいだ}</b>、{f|一度|いちど}も{f|実家|じっか}に{f|帰|かえ}らなかった。
I didn't go home once during my time studying abroad.
</pre>

That interval is over and closed; the sentence says nothing about today.

<pre>
{f|留学|りゅうがく}から{f|戻|もど}っ<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|一度|いちど}も{f|実家|じっか}に{f|帰|かえ}っていない。
I haven't been home once since I got back from studying abroad.
</pre>

Now the span is still running, which is why the verb has to be `ていない` rather
than `なかった`. That tense difference is the practical test: <code>{f|間|あいだ}</code>
takes a closed past, <code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> takes a present perfect.

### {f|以来|いらい} vs {f|以降|いこう} / {f|以後|いご}

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - all three mean "from that point on", but only
<code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> is locked to the past. <code>{f|以降|いこう}</code> and
<code>{f|以後|いご}</code> can point at the future.

</div>

<pre>
{f|来週|らいしゅう}<b>{f|以降|いこう}</b>なら、いつでも{f|大丈夫|だいじょうぶ}です。
Any time from next week onward is fine.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|去年|きょねん}の{f|事故|じこ}<b>{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|夜|よる}の{f|運転|うんてん}はしていない。
I haven't driven at night since last year's accident.
</pre>

Two further splits. <code>{f|以降|いこう}</code> and <code>{f|以後|いご}</code> attach to
nouns only - there is no <code>…て{f|以降|いこう}</code>. And
<code>{f|以後|いご}</code> used alone means "from now on", with a faint scolding
edge: 「{f|以後|いご}、{f|気|き}をつけます」 is what you say after being told off.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ {f|卒業|そつぎょう}する<b>{f|以来|いらい}</b> → ✅ {f|卒業|そつぎょう}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>
  A verb reaches <code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> through the て-form. The dictionary form
  never appears in front of it.

- ❌ {f|昼|ひる}ごはんを{f|食|た}べ<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|何|なに}も{f|飲|の}んでいない。 → ✅ {f|昼|ひる}ごはんを{f|食|た}べ<b>てから</b>、{f|何|なに}も{f|飲|の}んでいない。
  A few hours is far too short. <code>{f|以来|いらい}</code> needs a span worth
  remarking on.

- ❌ {f|毎朝|まいあさ}{f|起|お}き<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b> → ✅ {f|毎朝|まいあさ}{f|起|お}き<b>てから</b>
  The starting event has to be a one-off. A daily routine cannot be an origin
  point.

- ❌ {f|入学|にゅうがく}<b>の{f|以来|いらい}</b> → ✅ {f|入学|にゅうがく}<b>{f|以来|いらい}</b>
  A noun joins directly, with no `の`.

- ❌ {f|来月|らいげつ}{f|転職|てんしょく}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、{f|忙|いそが}しくなります。 → ✅ {f|来月|らいげつ}{f|転職|てんしょく}し<b>てから</b>、{f|忙|いそが}しくなります。
  The origin has to be behind you already. There is no future
  <code>{f|以来|いらい}</code>.

## Practice

**1.** {f|去年|きょねん}の{f|夏|なつ}に{f|喧嘩|けんか}し____、{f|彼|かれ}とは{f|一度|いちど}も{f|話|はな}していない。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|喧嘩|けんか}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>

One definite past quarrel opens the span, and {f|一度|いちど}も…ていない reports the
unbroken state that has held from then until now - the exact shape
<code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> exists for.

</details>

**2.** Choose: うがいをし（てから・て{f|以来|いらい}）、{f|10分|じゅっぷん}{f|待|ま}ってください。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

うがいをし<b>てから</b>、{f|10分|じゅっぷん}{f|待|ま}ってください。

The sentence is an instruction about the next few minutes.
<code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> can neither look forward nor cover so short a stretch,
and it cannot sit under a request.

</details>

**3.** Translate into Japanese: "I haven't touched a piano since I gave up lessons in high school."

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|高校|こうこう}でレッスンをやめ<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、ピアノに{f|触|さわ}っていない。

Quitting is a single past event, and the main clause is a negative perfect running
to the present, so <code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> is the natural choice over
`てから`.

</details>

**4.** Find and fix the error: {f|会社|かいしゃ}を{f|辞|や}めて{f|以来|いらい}、{f|去年|きょねん}{f|新|あたら}しい{f|仕事|しごと}を{f|見|み}つけました。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

✅ {f|会社|かいしゃ}を{f|辞|や}め<b>てから</b>、{f|去年|きょねん}{f|新|あたら}しい{f|仕事|しごと}を{f|見|み}つけました。

The main clause is a one-off event pinned to its own date, not a description of the
whole span. <code>て{f|以来|いらい}</code> wants a state, a habit or a "not once";
plain sequencing is `てから`.

</details>

**5.** Explain the difference in nuance:
(a) {f|入院|にゅういん}し<b>てから</b>、たばこをやめました。
(b) {f|入院|にゅういん}し<b>て{f|以来|いらい}</b>、たばこを{f|吸|す}っていません。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) simply puts the two events in order: hospital first, giving up smoking after.
It reports a decision, and says nothing about whether it stuck.

(b) treats the hospital stay as an origin point and describes the entire stretch
since. The `ていません` is doing real work - the claim is that the whole period from
then until this moment has been smoke-free.

</details>
