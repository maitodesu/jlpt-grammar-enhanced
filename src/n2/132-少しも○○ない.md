# 少しも～ない

**Meaning** · not one bit; not even a little - denies the smallest amount anyone could have expected

{f|少|すこ}しも is {f|少|すこ}し ("a little") with `も` clamped on the end, and that
`も` is what forces the negative. The literal shape is "not even a little", and the
force of the sentence comes from how modest the amount being denied is: the speaker
picks the smallest quantity imaginable and says even *that* did not happen.

**What is denied: the quantity, at its lowest setting.** This matters when you set
it beside the N3 pair. <code>とは{f|限|かぎ}らない</code> denies that a rule holds
every time; <code>わけではない</code> denies an inference. {f|少|すこ}しも denies the
thing itself, and does it by ruling out even the token amount that would have let
the claim survive.

## Formation

{f|少|すこ}しも is an adverb. It does not conjugate, it goes before the predicate,
and the predicate is negative.

| Attaches to | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Verb (plain) | {f|少|すこ}しも + ない-form | {f|変|か}わる → {f|少|すこ}しも{f|変|か}わらない |
| Verb (polite) | {f|少|すこ}しも + ません | {f|触|ふ}れる → {f|少|すこ}しも{f|触|ふ}れませんでした |
| い-adjective | {f|少|すこ}しも + ～くない | {f|痛|いた}い → {f|少|すこ}しも{f|痛|いた}くない |
| な-adjective・Noun | {f|少|すこ}しも + ～ではない | {f|平気|へいき} → {f|少|すこ}しも{f|平気|へいき}ではない |
| Existence | {f|少|すこ}しも + ない | {f|余裕|よゆう} → {f|余裕|よゆう}が{f|少|すこ}しもない |
| Literary | {f|少|すこ}しも + ～ず | {f|動|うご}く → {f|少|すこ}しも{f|動|うご}かず |
| Same family, counted | {f|一|ひと}つも／{f|一人|ひとり}も／{f|何|なに}も + negative | {f|残|のこ}る → {f|一|ひと}つも{f|残|のこ}っていない |

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - the `も` is not decoration. Drop it and the sentence stops being
a denial of everything and becomes a statement about a small amount. Every member
of this family - {f|一|ひと}つも, {f|一人|ひとり}も, {f|何|なに}も, {f|決|けっ}して -
behaves the same way: quantity word plus `も` plus a negative.

</div>

</pre>

</pre>

<pre>
{f|三日|みっか}も{f|降|ふ}り{f|続|つづ}いたのに、{f|川|かわ}の{f|水|みず}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも</b>{f|増|ふ}えて<b>いない</b>。

</pre>

<pre>

The person in charge said nothing whatsoever about the reason for the price rise.
</pre>

<pre>

</pre>

## Nuance & register

{f|少|すこ}しも is comfortable in both speech and writing. It is a shade more
emphatic than {f|全|まった}く and noticeably more careful than ちっとも, so it fits
a written argument, a formal complaint or a considered spoken remark. Nothing about
it is old-fashioned, though the bare literary form
<code>{f|少|すこ}しも{f|動|うご}かず</code> does belong to narration rather than
conversation.

What gives the word its colour is the implied concession. Because the speaker has
gone down to "even a little", the sentence quietly acknowledges that a little would
have been reasonable to expect. 「{f|少|すこ}しも{f|悪|わる}いと{f|思|おも}っていない」
is therefore sharper than 「{f|全|まった}く{f|悪|わる}いと{f|思|おも}っていない」: it
says not even a flicker of remorse, when a flicker was the minimum anyone was
hoping for.

It also appears in a softer, concessive shape that learners often misparse:
<code>{f|少|すこ}しも～ないわけではない</code>, "it is not as though there is none at
all". That is two denials stacked, and it comes out as a grudging admission - the
same territory as なくはない.

## Contrast

### {f|少|すこ}しも vs {f|少|すこ}し

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - {f|少|すこ}し is a quantity. {f|少|すこ}しも is a **denial of any
quantity**. The `も` is the whole difference.

</div>

<pre>
この{f|文章|ぶんしょう}は<b>{f|少|すこ}し</b>{f|分|わ}かりにくい。
This passage is a little hard to follow.
</pre>

<pre>
この{f|文章|ぶんしょう}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも</b>{f|分|わ}から<b>ない</b>。
I cannot make out a word of this passage.
</pre>

Putting {f|少|すこ}し directly in front of a negative does not produce "not much";
it produces something that sounds unfinished, because {f|少|すこ}し wants to measure
a positive amount. If you want "not much", the word is あまり.

### {f|少|すこ}しも vs {f|全|まった}く

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - both reach zero. {f|全|まった}く measures **the whole**;
{f|少|すこ}しも measures **the smallest part**, so it sounds more insistent when the
listener expected at least something.

</div>

<pre>
{f|彼女|かのじょ}は{f|事件|じけん}のことを<b>{f|全|まった}く</b>{f|話|はな}さ<b>なかった</b>。
She said nothing at all about the incident.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|彼女|かのじょ}は{f|事件|じけん}のことを<b>{f|少|すこ}しも</b>{f|話|はな}そうとし<b>なかった</b>。
She would not talk about the incident even a little.
</pre>

In most sentences either works and the difference is emphasis. Where they part is
the affirmative side: {f|全|まった}く has a second life in
<code>{f|全|まった}く{f|同|おな}じ</code> and <code>{f|全|まった}くの{f|偶然|ぐうぜん}</code>,
while {f|少|すこ}しも has none. {f|少|すこ}しも is negative-only, always.

### {f|少|すこ}しも～ない vs ～なくはない

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - {f|少|すこ}しも～ない is **zero**. ～なくはない is **a reluctant
minimum**, which is what you say when zero would be a lie.

</div>

<pre>
{f|興味|きょうみ}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しもない</b>。
I have no interest in it whatsoever.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|興味|きょうみ}が<b>なくはない</b>が、{f|時間|じかん}がない。
It is not that I have no interest - I just do not have the time.
</pre>

These are the two ends of the same scale, and choosing wrongly is socially
expensive. The first closes a topic; the second keeps it alive while declining. Say
{f|少|すこ}しもない to a colleague's proposal and you have refused the person as well
as the idea.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ {f|事情|じじょう}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも{f|分|わ}かる</b>。 → ✅ {f|事情|じじょう}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも{f|分|わ}からない</b>。
  {f|少|すこ}しも cannot stand in front of an affirmative at all. Unlike
  {f|全然|ぜんぜん}, it has no colloquial escape hatch.

- ❌ {f|注意|ちゅうい}されても<b>{f|少|すこ}し{f|気|き}にしていない</b>。 → ✅ <b>{f|少|すこ}しも{f|気|き}にしていない</b>。
  Without `も` the adverb tries to measure an amount of not-caring, and the sentence
  falls apart. The `も` is what licenses the negative.

- ❌ ゆうべは<b>{f|少|すこ}しも{f|眠|ねむ}れなかった</b>が、{f|二時間|にじかん}は{f|寝|ね}た。 → ✅ ゆうべは<b>あまり{f|眠|ねむ}れなかった</b>が、{f|二時間|にじかん}は{f|寝|ね}た。
  Two hours is not zero. {f|少|すこ}しも rules out even a token amount, so it cannot
  be walked back in the next clause.

- ❌ <b>{f|少|すこ}しもの{f|問題|もんだい}もない</b>。 → ✅ <b>{f|何|なん}の{f|問題|もんだい}もない</b>。
  {f|少|すこ}しも is an adverb and never takes の to modify a noun. For "not the
  slightest X", the noun frame is <code>{f|何|なん}の + Noun + も + negative</code>.

## Practice

**1.** {f|一時間|いちじかん}{f|話|はな}し{f|合|あ}ったが、{f|意見|いけん}の{f|差|さ}は____{f|縮|ちぢ}まらなかった。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|意見|いけん}の{f|差|さ}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも</b>{f|縮|ちぢ}まらなかった。

An hour of talking is exactly the effort that should have narrowed the gap by at
least a little, and {f|少|すこ}しも denies even that minimum. {f|全|まった}く would
also be correct and slightly flatter in tone.

</details>

**2.** Choose: この{f|案|あん}に（{f|少|すこ}しも{f|賛成|さんせい}できない・{f|賛成|さんせい}できなくはない）が、もう{f|少|すこ}し{f|時間|じかん}がほしい。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

この{f|案|あん}に<b>{f|賛成|さんせい}できなくはない</b>が、もう{f|少|すこ}し{f|時間|じかん}がほしい。

The が clause asks for more time, which only makes sense if some agreement is
possible. {f|少|すこ}しも{f|賛成|さんせい}できない refuses outright, and the second
half would then contradict it.

</details>

**3.** Translate into Japanese: "I apologised twice, and he did not soften one bit."

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|二回|にかい}{f|謝|あやま}ったのに、{f|彼|かれ}の{f|態度|たいど}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも</b>{f|変|か}わら<b>なかった</b>。

"One bit" after a genuine effort is the {f|少|すこ}しも slot. のに carries the sense
that the apologies should have had some effect, which is what makes the adverb bite.

</details>

**4.** Find and fix the error: あの{f|人|ひと}は{f|少|すこ}しもの{f|遠慮|えんりょ}がない。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

✅ あの{f|人|ひと}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも{f|遠慮|えんりょ}がない</b>。（or あの{f|人|ひと}には<b>{f|何|なん}の{f|遠慮|えんりょ}もない</b>）

{f|少|すこ}しも modifies the predicate, not the noun, so の has no business there.
Keep the adverb bare and let it reach the negative <code>ない</code> at the end.

</details>

**5.** Explain the difference in nuance:
(a) {f|彼|かれ}は<b>{f|少|すこ}しも</b>{f|悪|わる}いと{f|思|おも}って<b>いない</b>。
(b) {f|彼|かれ}が{f|悪|わる}いと{f|思|おも}っている<b>わけではない</b>。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) is about *his* state of mind and it is an accusation: not a flicker of remorse,
when a flicker was the least anyone expected. The smallness of {f|少|すこ}し is what
makes it cutting.

(b) is about *the speaker's* position, and it withdraws an inference rather than a
fact. Something the speaker said sounded like blame, and this sentence takes the
blame reading back without denying whatever happened.

</details>
