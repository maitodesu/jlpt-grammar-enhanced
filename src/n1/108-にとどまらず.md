# ～にとどまらず

**Meaning** · it does not stop at A - the thing spread past a boundary you would have expected to hold it

とどまる is "to stay within, to stop at", and `にとどまらず` is its written negative:
the influence, the damage, the popularity, the argument did not stay where it
started. A is not simply one item on a list. It is a **limit** - a place, a field,
a group, a period - and the sentence is about that limit being crossed.

## Formation

Nouns are the normal input; a dictionary-form verb is also possible. The second
clause almost always carries も, まで or {f|及|およ}ぶ.

| Attaches to | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Noun | Noun + にとどまらず | {f|国内|こくない}にとどまらず |
| Verb | dictionary form + にとどまらず | {f|指摘|してき}する → {f|指摘|してき}するにとどまらず |
| な-adjective | ～である + にとどまらず | {f|便利|べんり}であるにとどまらず |
| Second clause | ～にも／～にまで + {f|及|およ}ぶ・{f|広|ひろ}がる | …{f|海外|かいがい}にまで{f|及|およ}んだ |
| Sentence-final | ～にとどまらない | {f|影響|えいきょう}は{f|一社|いっしゃ}にとどまらない |
| Affirmative counterpart | ～にとどまる | {f|被害|ひがい}は{f|一部|いちぶ}にとどまった |

<div class="note">

**Note** - the affirmative <code>にとどまる</code> is worth learning alongside it,
because news writing uses the two as a pair.
<code>{f|死者|ししゃ}は{f|出|で}ず、けが{f|人|にん}{f|三名|さんめい}にとどまった</code>
means the damage was contained at that figure. Recognising the positive makes the
negative obvious.

</div>

## Examples

<pre>
このアニメの{f|人気|にんき}は{f|若者|わかもの}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|親|おや}の{f|世代|せだい}にも{f|広|ひろ}がっている。
The show's popularity is not confined to the young; it has spread to their parents' generation.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|被害|ひがい}は{f|一社|いっしゃ}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|業界|ぎょうかい}{f|全体|ぜんたい}に{f|及|およ}んだ。
The damage did not stop at one company but reached the industry as a whole.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|彼|かれ}の{f|活動|かつどう}は{f|音楽|おんがく}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|映画|えいが}や{f|舞台|ぶたい}にも{f|広|ひろ}がった。
His work has gone beyond music into film and the stage.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|停電|ていでん}の{f|影響|えいきょう}は{f|工場|こうじょう}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|周辺|しゅうへん}の{f|住宅|じゅうたく}にまで{f|及|およ}んだ。
The effects of the power cut were not limited to the plant; they extended to homes in the area.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|値上|ねあ}げは{f|電気|でんき}{f|代|だい}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|食品|しょくひん}や{f|交通費|こうつうひ}にも{f|広|ひろ}がりつつある。
The rises are no longer confined to electricity; they are spreading to food and fares.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|会議|かいぎ}では{f|問題|もんだい}を{f|指摘|してき}する<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|具体的|ぐたいてき}な{f|対策|たいさく}まで{f|示|しめ}された。
The meeting did not merely identify the problem; concrete measures were put forward.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|支援|しえん}は{f|物資|ぶっし}を{f|送|おく}る<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|現地|げんち}での{f|住宅|じゅうたく}{f|再建|さいけん}にまで{f|及|およ}んでいる。
The aid goes beyond sending supplies as far as rebuilding housing on the ground.
</pre>

<pre>
この{f|研究|けんきゅう}の{f|意義|いぎ}は、{f|学問|がくもん}の{f|世界|せかい}<b>にとどまらない</b>だろう。
The significance of this research will not, I think, remain within the academic world.
</pre>

## Nuance & register

Written and formal, and completely current: newspapers, company reports, academic
prose, television commentary, the body of a briefing. It is not literary in the way
`のみか` is - a manager giving a presentation says it without ceremony - but you
would not use it about your own weekend.

What it needs is **scale**. The A slot has to be something a reader could have
imagined as a boundary. {f|国内|こくない}, {f|一部|いちぶ}の{f|社員|しゃいん},
{f|昨年|さくねん}, {f|音楽|おんがく}の{f|分野|ぶんや} all work. A single object does
not: 「{f|私|わたし}は{f|英語|えいご}にとどまらず、{f|中国語|ちゅうごくご}も{f|勉強|べんきょう}している」
sounds absurdly grand, because one person's language study is not a frontier that
anything overflows. That sentence wants `だけでなく`.

The tone is usually **serious and often unwelcome**. Damage spreading, criticism
spreading, price rises spreading - these are its commonest subjects, and the
sentence carries a faint alarm. It handles good news perfectly well
({f|人気|にんき} spreading, an artist's range widening), but even then the feeling
is of something growing past its container.

<code>{f|単|たん}に…にとどまらず</code> is a common intensification, and marks the
first item explicitly as the smaller, more obvious one.

## Contrast

### ～にとどまらず vs ～だけでなく

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - `だけでなく` **adds** B to A. `にとどまらず` says A was a
**limit** and the thing went past it.

</div>

<pre>
この{f|店|みせ}は{f|安|やす}い<b>だけでなく</b>、{f|品|しな}ぞろえもいい。
This shop isn't just cheap; it has a good range too.
</pre>

<pre>
この{f|店|みせ}の{f|評判|ひょうばん}は{f|近所|きんじょ}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|市外|しがい}からも{f|客|きゃく}を{f|集|あつ}めている。
The shop's reputation has spread beyond the neighbourhood and is drawing customers from outside the city.
</pre>

Cheapness and range are two qualities side by side, so `だけでなく` fits and
`にとどまらず` would be nonsense - cheapness is not a boundary. The second sentence
has a real edge to cross, and `だけでなく` there would lose the sense of spreading
outward.

### ～にとどまらず vs ～に{f|限|かぎ}らず

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - <code>に{f|限|かぎ}らず</code> says A was **never the only
case**. `にとどまらず` says it **started** at A and has since gone further.

</div>

<pre>
{f|土日|どにち}<b>に{f|限|かぎ}らず</b>、{f|平日|へいじつ}も{f|混|こ}んでいます。
It's busy on weekdays too, not just at weekends.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|混雑|こんざつ}は{f|土日|どにち}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|平日|へいじつ}にまで{f|広|ひろ}がってきた。
The crowding is no longer just at weekends; it has spread into the working week.
</pre>

The first is a standing fact, and could have been true for years. The second
describes a change over time: it used to be weekends, and now it is not. That is
the whole difference, and it is a difference of **movement**.

### ～にとどまらず vs ～はもとより

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - `はもとより` treats A as **too obvious to argue about** and adds
B. `にとどまらず` treats A as a **boundary** and reports that it failed to hold.

</div>

<pre>
{f|新型|しんがた}は{f|性能|せいのう}<b>はもとより</b>、デザインも{f|評価|ひょうか}されている。
The new model is praised for its design as well as, of course, its performance.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|議論|ぎろん}は{f|価格|かかく}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|会社|かいしゃ}の{f|方針|ほうしん}そのものに{f|及|およ}んだ。
The discussion went beyond price to the company's policy itself.
</pre>

`はもとより` is about what the reader already grants; it takes A for granted so that
B can be the news. `にとどまらず` is about extent, and both halves are news. They
overlap in translation - both come out as "not only" - and pull apart the moment
you ask whether A is *obvious* or *a limit*.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ {f|私|わたし}は{f|英語|えいご}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|中国語|ちゅうごくご}も{f|習|なら}っています。 → ✅ {f|私|わたし}は{f|英語|えいご}<b>だけでなく</b>、{f|中国語|ちゅうごくご}も{f|習|なら}っています。
  One learner's two languages are not a boundary being overflowed. The pattern
  needs a scope big enough to be crossed.

- ❌ {f|影響|えいきょう}は{f|国内|こくない}に<b>とどまらないで</b>、{f|海外|かいがい}にも{f|及|およ}んだ。 → ✅ {f|影響|えいきょう}は{f|国内|こくない}に<b>とどまらず</b>、{f|海外|かいがい}にも{f|及|およ}んだ。
  This is a written pattern and takes the written negative `ず`. `ないで` drops the
  sentence two registers and does not join clauses this way.

- ❌ この{f|制度|せいど}は{f|便利|べんり}な<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|費用|ひよう}も{f|安|やす}い。 → ✅ この{f|制度|せいど}は{f|便利|べんり}である<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|費用|ひよう}も{f|安|やす}い。
  A な-adjective needs `である` before に. In practice a plain
  <code>{f|便利|べんり}なだけでなく</code> is the more natural sentence anyway.

- ❌ {f|世界|せかい}{f|中|じゅう}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|日本|にほん}でも{f|話題|わだい}になっている。 → ✅ {f|日本|にほん}{f|国内|こくない}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|世界|せかい}{f|中|じゅう}で{f|話題|わだい}になっている。
  The movement is outward. Putting the wider scope first makes the second clause an
  anticlimax.

## Practice

**1.** {f|地震|じしん}の{f|被害|ひがい}は{f|建物|たてもの}____、{f|水道|すいどう}や{f|通信|つうしん}にも{f|及|およ}んでいる。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|建物|たてもの}<b>にとどまらず</b>

Buildings are the expected extent of earthquake damage, and the sentence reports
that it went further. Note {f|及|およ}ぶ in the second clause - the verb this
pattern most often ends up with.

</details>

**2.** Choose: この{f|大学|だいがく}は{f|研究|けんきゅう}{f|環境|かんきょう}（にとどまらず・だけでなく）、{f|寮|りょう}の{f|設備|せつび}も{f|整|ととの}っている。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|研究|けんきゅう}{f|環境|かんきょう}<b>だけでなく</b>

Two good points about one university, listed side by side. Neither is a boundary,
and nothing is spreading, so `にとどまらず` would sound inflated.

</details>

**3.** Translate into Japanese: "The shortage is no longer confined to some regions; it has now reached the whole country."

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|不足|ふそく}は{f|一部|いちぶ}の{f|地域|ちいき}<b>にとどまらず</b>、{f|全国|ぜんこく}に{f|広|ひろ}がっている。

"No longer confined to" is the pattern's exact sense, and the second clause
supplies the wider scope. Note the reading of {f|不足|ふそく} - the ふ does not
voice here, unlike {f|準備|じゅんび}{f|不足|ぶそく} where it does.

</details>

**4.** Find and fix the error: {f|彼|かれ}の{f|発言|はつげん}は{f|社内|しゃない}にとどまらないで、SNSでも{f|問題|もんだい}になった。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

✅ {f|彼|かれ}の{f|発言|はつげん}は{f|社内|しゃない}<b>にとどまらず</b>、SNSでも{f|問題|もんだい}になった。

The pattern is fixed in its written form. `とどまらないで` is the spoken negative
te-form and cannot stand in for `ず` in a sentence with this weight.

</details>

**5.** Explain the difference in nuance:
(a) {f|残業|ざんぎょう}は{f|一部|いちぶ}の{f|部署|ぶしょ}に{f|限|かぎ}らず、{f|会社|かいしゃ}{f|全体|ぜんたい}の{f|問題|もんだい}だ。
(b) {f|残業|ざんぎょう}は{f|一部|いちぶ}の{f|部署|ぶしょ}にとどまらず、{f|会社|かいしゃ}{f|全体|ぜんたい}に{f|広|ひろ}がっている。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) corrects a belief. Someone thinks overtime is a problem in one or two
departments, and the speaker says it never was - it is company-wide and always has
been.

(b) describes a development. It did begin in a few departments, and it has since
spread. The first is about the truth of a claim; the second is about a process
still under way.

</details>
