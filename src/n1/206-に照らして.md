# ～に照らして

**Meaning** · in the light of; measured against - holds the matter up against a law, a standard or a body of past experience, and reaches a verdict

<code>{f|照|て}らす</code> is "to shine a light on something". The noun in front is the
**yardstick** you shine: a statute, a set of criteria, previous cases, what you
personally have seen over the years. What follows is almost always a judgement -
{f|違法|いほう}だ, {f|妥当|だとう}だ, {f|認|みと}められない,
{f|問題|もんだい}がある, {f|判断|はんだん}する. The pattern does not describe an action
being carried out; it describes something being assessed.

## Formation

<code>{f|照|て}らす</code> is a う-verb ({f|五段|ごだん}/godan/Group 1). The yardstick
is marked with `に`, and the pattern takes a **noun** only.

| Attaches to | Form | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Noun | Noun + に{f|照|て}らして | {f|法|ほう} → {f|法|ほう}に{f|照|て}らして |
| Noun (stiffer, documents) | Noun + に{f|照|て}らし | {f|規定|きてい} → {f|規定|きてい}に{f|照|て}らし |
| Conditional | Noun + に{f|照|て}らすと / に{f|照|て}らせば | {f|判例|はんれい} → {f|判例|はんれい}に{f|照|て}らせば |
| Modifying a following noun | Noun + に{f|照|て}らした + Noun | {f|基準|きじゅん} → {f|基準|きじゅん}に{f|照|て}らした{f|評価|ひょうか} |
| Cross-checking two things | A を B に{f|照|て}らし{f|合|あ}わせる | {f|証言|しょうげん} → {f|証言|しょうげん}を{f|記録|きろく}に{f|照|て}らし{f|合|あ}わせる |
| Set phrase | {f|経験|けいけん}に{f|照|て}らして | - → {f|経験|けいけん}に{f|照|て}らして{f|言|い}えば |

<div class="note">

**Note** - <code>{f|照|て}らし{f|合|あ}わせる</code> in the fifth row is a related but
separate verb, and it is the one you want when two documents are being compared with
each other rather than one thing being judged against a standard. It is also far more
usable in ordinary working Japanese than <code>に{f|照|て}らして</code> itself.

</div>

## Examples

<pre>
その{f|行為|こうい}は{f|法|ほう}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|明|あき}らかに{f|違法|いほう}だ。
Measured against the law, that act is plainly illegal.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|過去|かこ}の{f|例|れい}<b>に{f|照|て}らすと</b>、{f|来月|らいげつ}には{f|落|お}ち{f|着|つ}くはずだ。
Judging by past cases, things should settle down next month.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|私|わたし}の{f|経験|けいけん}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|言|い}えば、この{f|方法|ほうほう}はうまくいかない。
Speaking from my own experience, this approach will not work.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|評価|ひょうか}{f|基準|きじゅん}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>、{f|今回|こんかい}の{f|申請|しんせい}は{f|認|みと}められません。
Measured against the assessment criteria, this application cannot be approved.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|社内|しゃない}の{f|規定|きてい}<b>に{f|照|て}らし</b>、{f|処分|しょぶん}の{f|内容|ないよう}を{f|決|き}めた。
The content of the disciplinary measure was determined against the internal regulations.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|証言|しょうげん}を{f|当時|とうじ}の{f|記録|きろく}<b>に{f|照|て}らし{f|合|あ}わせた</b>ところ、{f|食|く}い{f|違|ちが}いが{f|見|み}つかった。
When the testimony was checked against the records of the time, discrepancies emerged.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|判例|はんれい}<b>に{f|照|て}らせば</b>、この{f|契約|けいやく}{f|条項|じょうこう}は{f|無効|むこう}と{f|判断|はんだん}される{f|可能性|かのうせい}が{f|高|たか}い。
In the light of precedent, this contract clause is likely to be held void.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|当時|とうじ}の{f|常識|じょうしき}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|考|かんが}えれば、{f|彼|かれ}の{f|判断|はんだん}だけを{f|責|せ}めることはできない。
Judged by the accepted thinking of the day, his decision alone cannot be blamed.
</pre>

## Nuance & register

This is courtroom, committee-room and audit-report Japanese. You meet it in
judgements, in disciplinary findings, in inspection reports, in the part of a formal
speech where a decision is being justified. Outside those settings it sounds
theatrical: nobody says
<code>{f|時計|とけい}に{f|照|て}らして{f|遅刻|ちこく}だ</code>. In conversation the same
thought comes out as <code>{f|規則|きそく}で{f|考|かんが}えると</code>,
<code>{f|前|まえ}の{f|例|れい}から{f|見|み}ると</code> or just
<code>{f|今|いま}までの{f|感|かん}じだと</code>.

One shape does survive into ordinary speech:
<code>{f|経験|けいけん}に{f|照|て}らして</code>, and especially
<code>{f|経験|けいけん}に{f|照|て}らして{f|言|い}えば</code>. A senior colleague will
use it in a meeting to introduce an opinion, and it lends the opinion the weight of
years. Said by someone in their twenties it tends to sound as though they are
imitating a manager - a tendency, not a rule, but worth knowing before you try it.

Because the pattern promises a verdict, a sentence that opens with it and then fails
to deliver one feels unfinished. The sentence
<code>{f|基準|きじゅん}に{f|照|て}らして{f|書類|しょるい}を{f|集|あつ}めた</code> reads
oddly; collecting documents is not a judgement.

## Contrast

### ～に{f|照|て}らして vs ～に{f|基|もと}づいて

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - <code>～に{f|基|もと}づいて</code> takes its authority as the
**source** of the action. <code>～に{f|照|て}らして</code> takes it as the
**measuring stick** and delivers a verdict.

</div>

<pre>
{f|条例|じょうれい}<b>に{f|基|もと}づいて</b>{f|指導|しどう}を{f|行|おこな}った。
Guidance was issued under the ordinance.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|条例|じょうれい}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|問題|もんだい}があると{f|判断|はんだん}した。
Measured against the ordinance, it was judged to be problematic.
</pre>

In the first, the ordinance empowers the action; in the second, it grades it. This is
why <code>{f|基|もと}づいて</code> is followed by verbs of *doing* -
{f|処分|しょぶん}する, {f|作成|さくせい}する, {f|支給|しきゅう}する - and
<code>{f|照|て}らして</code> by verbs and adjectives of *assessing*. The two can
appear in the same paragraph without contradiction, because judging a case and then
acting on the finding are different steps.

### ～に{f|照|て}らして vs ～に{f|即|そく}して

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - the direction is reversed.
<code>～に{f|即|そく}して</code> bends the **standard** to fit the reality;
<code>～に{f|照|て}らして</code> measures the **reality** against a fixed standard.

</div>

<pre>
{f|現場|げんば}の{f|実態|じったい}<b>に{f|即|そく}した</b>{f|基準|きじゅん}を{f|作|つく}る。
We will draw up a standard that fits conditions on the ground.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|基準|きじゅん}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|現場|げんば}の{f|実態|じったい}を{f|評価|ひょうか}する。
We will evaluate conditions on the ground against the standard.
</pre>

The same two nouns, the same relationship, opposite ends of it. If the standard is
the thing that has to give, you want <code>{f|即|そく}して</code>. If the standard is
fixed and the situation is on trial, you want <code>{f|照|て}らして</code>.

### ～に{f|照|て}らして vs ～と{f|比|くら}べて

<div class="rule">

**Rule of thumb** - <code>～と{f|比|くら}べて</code> compares two things of the same
kind and reports a difference. <code>～に{f|照|て}らして</code> requires the noun to be
a **criterion**, and reports a judgement.

</div>

<pre>
{f|去年|きょねん}<b>と{f|比|くら}べて</b>{f|売|う}り{f|上|あ}げが{f|落|お}ちた。
Sales are down compared with last year.
</pre>

<pre>
{f|目標|もくひょう}{f|額|がく}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|売|う}り{f|上|あ}げは{f|十分|じゅうぶん}とは{f|言|い}えない。
Measured against the target figure, sales cannot be called adequate.
</pre>

Last year's sales are a fact you sit beside this year's; the target is a bar the
figure either clears or does not. That is why
❌<code>{f|弟|おとうと}に{f|照|て}らして{f|背|せ}が{f|高|たか}い</code> is impossible - a
brother is not a criterion - while
<code>{f|平均|へいきん}{f|身長|しんちょう}に{f|照|て}らして</code> is at least
conceivable, if stiff.

## Common mistakes

- ❌ {f|弟|おとうと}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|背|せ}が{f|高|たか}い。 → ✅ {f|弟|おとうと}<b>と{f|比|くら}べて</b>{f|背|せ}が{f|高|たか}い。
  The noun before this pattern has to be a standard, not just the other item in a
  comparison.

- ❌ {f|法律|ほうりつ}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|許可|きょか}を{f|出|だ}した。 → ✅ {f|法律|ほうりつ}<b>に{f|基|もと}づいて</b>{f|許可|きょか}を{f|出|だ}した。
  Issuing a permit is an action taken *under* the law, not a verdict measured against
  it. Only <code>{f|基|もと}づいて</code> fits.

- ❌ {f|基準|きじゅん}<b>を{f|照|て}らして</b> → ✅ {f|基準|きじゅん}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>
  The standard is marked with `に`. `を` belongs to the literal verb -
  <code>{f|懐中|かいちゅう}{f|電灯|でんとう}で{f|足元|あしもと}を{f|照|て}らす</code>,
  shining a torch on your feet.

- ❌ {f|規定|きてい}に<b>{f|照|しょう}らして</b> → ✅ {f|規定|きてい}に<b>{f|照|て}らして</b>
  <code>照</code> is ショウ in {f|参照|さんしょう} and {f|照明|しょうめい}, but the verb is
  {f|照|て}らす, and らして is okurigana outside the furigana group.

## Practice

**1.** {f|今回|こんかい}の{f|発言|はつげん}は、{f|社内|しゃない}の{f|行動|こうどう}{f|指針|ししん}____{f|不適切|ふてきせつ}だと{f|判断|はんだん}された。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|行動|こうどう}{f|指針|ししん}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|不適切|ふてきせつ}だと{f|判断|はんだん}された

A code of conduct is a criterion, and the sentence delivers a verdict on the remark.
<code>に{f|照|て}らし</code> would fit an internal document just as well.

</details>

**2.** Choose: {f|就業|しゅうぎょう}{f|規則|きそく}（に{f|照|て}らして・に{f|基|もと}づいて）{f|残業|ざんぎょう}{f|代|だい}を{f|支給|しきゅう}する。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|就業|しゅうぎょう}{f|規則|きそく}<b>に{f|基|もと}づいて</b>{f|支給|しきゅう}する。

Paying overtime is an action the rules authorise, not a judgement about whether
something measures up. Change the verb to
<code>{f|支給|しきゅう}{f|対象|たいしょう}かどうかを{f|判断|はんだん}する</code> and
<code>{f|照|て}らして</code> becomes the natural choice.

</details>

**3.** Translate into Japanese: "In the light of past cases, a decision is unlikely to come this year."

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

{f|過去|かこ}の{f|事例|じれい}<b>に{f|照|て}らせば</b>、{f|今年|ことし}{f|中|じゅう}に{f|結論|けつろん}が{f|出|で}ることはないだろう。

The conditional <code>に{f|照|て}らせば</code> is the usual shape when the yardstick
supports a prediction rather than a ruling. Note that in {f|今年|ことし}{f|中|じゅう} the character
<code>中</code> is じゅう, not ちゅう.

</details>

**4.** Find and fix the error: {f|基準|きじゅん}に{f|照|て}らして、{f|必要|ひつよう}な{f|書類|しょるい}を{f|集|あつ}めた。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

✅ {f|基準|きじゅん}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>、{f|書類|しょるい}が{f|足|た}りているかどうかを{f|確|たし}かめた。

The original stops short of a judgement, and collecting documents is an ordinary
action - <code>{f|基準|きじゅん}に{f|従|したが}って{f|集|あつ}めた</code> would say
that. This pattern needs an assessment on the other side of it.

</details>

**5.** Explain the difference in nuance:
(a) {f|時代|じだい}の{f|流|なが}れ<b>に{f|即|そく}して</b>{f|校則|こうそく}を{f|見|み}{f|直|なお}す。
(b) {f|今|いま}の{f|常識|じょうしき}<b>に{f|照|て}らして</b>{f|校則|こうそく}を{f|見|み}{f|直|なお}す。

<details><summary>Answer</summary>

(a) says the school rules are to be reshaped so that they fit how the world now is.
The rules are the thing being altered, and the times are simply the shape they must
take.

(b) says today's common sense is being used as a measuring stick, and each rule will
be tested against it - some passing, some failing. (b) implies a rule-by-rule
verdict; (a) implies a redesign.

</details>
